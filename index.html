A real-time ASL fingerspelling translator

Built by DayoOn-device. By design.

01 / The gap

Not everyone can read sign language.

  • Interpreters are not always there when a conversation needs one.
  • Many translators upload video of you to a server somewhere.

SignBridge is an assistive step toward connection, not a replacement for interpreters.

02 / The demo

See it work.

One hand. One webcam.
One letter at a time.

03 / The process

Camera to speech
in four steps.

  1. 01

    Camera

    Webcam frames, captured with OpenCV.

  2. 02

    Hand tracking

    MediaPipe finds 21 landmarks on the hand.

  3. 03

    Classifier

    A small neural network recognizes 24 static letters.

  4. 04

    Output

    Smoothing builds letters into words, shown and spoken aloud.

04 / Local by design

Runs on the device,
not in the cloud.

  • Video never leaves the room.
  • No internet connection needed.
